About Religious Tourism in India

Religious tourism refers to travelling to sacred places for worship, pilgrimage, spiritual learning and cultural experiences. India is one of the world's most diverse religious tourism destinations, attracting visitors from different parts of the world.


From Varanasi and Ayodhya to Bodh Gaya and the Golden Temple, India's religious destinations represent centuries of faith, tradition, architecture and cultural heritage.

Famous Religious Tourism Destinations

Explore some of India's most important religious and spiritual destinations.

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Varanasi is one of India's oldest living cities, famous for its sacred ghats, Ganga Aarti and spiritual traditions.

Golden Temple, Amritsar

Sri Harmandir Sahib is an important Sikh pilgrimage centre known for its spiritual atmosphere and community kitchen.

Bodh Gaya, Bihar

Bodh Gaya is a major Buddhist pilgrimage destination associated with the enlightenment of Gautama Buddha.

Ajmer Sharif, Rajasthan

The Dargah of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti is an important spiritual destination visited by people of different faiths.

Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh

Tirupati is famous for the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple and attracts millions of pilgrims every year.

Old Goa, Goa

Old Goa is known for its historic churches and religious monuments that reflect India's Christian heritage.

Responsible Religious Tourism

Sustainable religious tourism ensures that religious destinations remain clean, accessible and culturally meaningful for future generations.


  • Respect local religious customs and traditions.
  • Avoid plastic waste and maintain cleanliness.
  • Use public transport whenever possible.
  • Support local communities and small businesses.
  • Follow crowd-management and safety guidelines.
  • Protect historical and religious monuments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is religious tourism?

Religious tourism involves travelling to sacred places for worship, pilgrimage, spiritual experiences and cultural learning.

Which are the famous religious places in India?

Some famous destinations include Varanasi, Ayodhya, Tirupati, Bodh Gaya, Amritsar and Ajmer Sharif.

How can religious tourism become sustainable?

Through better waste management, public transportation, water conservation, responsible visitor behaviour and protection of cultural heritage.
